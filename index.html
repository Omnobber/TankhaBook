Everything from the gate to the salary slip

Attendance in one tap

Mark present, absent or half day for every worker, or mark everyone present and fix the exceptions. Holidays are built in.

Geo-fenced punch-in with selfie

Workers punch in from their own phone. The app checks they are inside the branch radius and can ask for a selfie.

Daily, monthly and piece-rate pay

Set the pay type per worker. Overtime, advances and piece counts flow straight into the month's salary.

PF and ESI, switched on or off

Turn deductions on for your unit and see the totals in the monthly report.

Leave, advance and bill requests

Workers send requests with a bill photo. You approve or reject in a tap, and the decision is recorded.

Many branches, one owner view

Add branches and departments, see cost for each, and send a broadcast message to all staff or one branch. Download the salary sheet as CSV.

Schools and colleges (preview)

Teachers mark student attendance in a tap, spot students below 75%, and send a message to a whole class or only to the parents who need it.

Three steps, every day

Mark

Supervisors mark attendance, overtime and pieces, or workers punch in themselves.

Review

Approve leave, advance and bill requests. The dashboard shows who is in and who is absent.

Pay

Open the month's report, download the salary sheet and send each worker their slip.

Questions owners ask first

Do my workers need to install anything?

No. Tankhabook opens in the phone's browser, so a link is enough.

Can I try it without giving my details?

Yes. The demo above runs on your device and needs no sign-up.

Does it handle contract and piece-rate workers?

Yes. Each worker can be on daily wage, monthly salary or piece rate.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Download the monthly salary sheet as CSV at any time.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Free covers up to 5 employees with basic attendance and simple payroll. No credit card is needed.

How long does setup take?

Add your workers and pay rates, then start marking attendance. Message us on WhatsApp and we will help you set up.

Does it work on a basic Android phone?

It opens in the phone browser, so there is no app to install. Try the demo above on your own phone.
